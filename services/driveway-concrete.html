Questions

Driveway & Concrete questions

Will pressure washing remove oil stains?

It lifts surface grime and newer stains. Old oil that has soaked deep into the concrete often lightens rather than disappearing completely, and we'll tell you what to expect before we start.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (1,300-3,100 PSI) for tough surfaces like concrete and driveways. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) with specialized cleaning solutions, making it safe for delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, and painted wood.
