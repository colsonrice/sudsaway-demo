House Washing

Siding, brick, and trim cleaned with a low-pressure soft wash.

Get a free estimate (708) 334-2685

Most of what makes a house look tired is organic growth: green algae on the shaded side, black mildew spots, and the dirt they hold onto. A soft wash uses cleaning solution at low pressure to kill that growth rather than blast it off, so the siding comes back clean without being forced apart or etched.

How we do it

  1. 1

    Walk the house with you and note anything delicate: light fixtures, outlets, open vents, and plantings.

  2. 2

    Pre-rinse landscaping and cover delicate plants.

  3. 3

    Apply the cleaning solution at low pressure.

  4. 4

    Give it time to break down algae, mildew, and dirt.

  5. 5

    Rinse the house, windows, and surrounding plants thoroughly.

Questions

House Washing questions

How often should I have my house washed?

We recommend exterior house washing every 1-2 years depending on your environment. Homes in shaded or humid areas may benefit from annual cleaning to prevent algae and mildew buildup.

Will a soft wash damage my siding?

No. Soft washing runs at low pressure (under 500 PSI) and lets the cleaning solution do the work, which is why it's the safe choice for vinyl, painted wood, and stucco.

What precautions do you take to protect plants and pets?

Our cleaning process uses professional-strength solutions that can be harsh on plants and pets. We take precautions by pre-rinsing landscaping, covering delicate plants, and asking that pets be kept inside during service. We always rinse treated areas thoroughly when finished.
