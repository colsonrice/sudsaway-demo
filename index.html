Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (1,300-3,100 PSI) for tough surfaces like concrete and driveways. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) with specialized cleaning solutions, making it safe for delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, and painted wood.

Is soft washing safe for my roof?

Absolutely. Soft washing is the method recommended by shingle manufacturers like GAF and CertainTeed. High-pressure washing can damage shingles and void your warranty. Our soft wash method cleans effectively without any risk to your roof.

How often should I have my house washed?

We recommend exterior house washing every 1-2 years depending on your environment. Homes in shaded or humid areas may benefit from annual cleaning to prevent algae and mildew buildup.

What precautions do you take to protect plants and pets?

Our cleaning process uses professional-strength solutions that can be harsh on plants and pets. We take precautions by pre-rinsing landscaping, covering delicate plants, and asking that pets be kept inside during service. We always rinse treated areas thoroughly when finished.
